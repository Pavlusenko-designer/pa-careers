    Why Work With Us?

    Our benefits package is designed to support your overall well-being, helping you thrive both at work and in life.

    • Financial Security: Retirement plans and financial planning resources.
    • Physical Health: Comprehensive health insurance and fitness incentives.
    • Mental Wellness: Counseling services and wellness programs.

    Our Commitment to Diversity & Inclusion

    At the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, we believe that a diverse and inclusive workplace is essential for innovation, growth, and the success of our public service mission. We are committed to fostering an environment where every employee feels valued, respected, and empowered to contribute their unique perspectives and talents. Our dedication to diversity, equity, and inclusion ensures that we not only reflect the communities we serve but also create a culture where all voices are heard and everyone has the opportunity to thrive.
