    Job description

    Make operations work better for Pennsylvanians

    The Commonwealth is looking for a Senior Operations Manager who can connect people, processes, and data to improve public service delivery. This role leads a small team and works with partners across agencies to make complex work more reliable, transparent, and easier to use.

    What you will do

    • Own operating plans, service-level goals, and improvement roadmaps for assigned programs.
    • Partner with program leaders to clarify priorities, risks, staffing needs, and delivery milestones.
    • Use qualitative feedback and operating data to identify barriers and recommend practical changes.
    • Build repeatable processes, decision records, and reporting that help teams move with confidence.
    • Coach team members, support inclusive collaboration, and communicate progress to senior leaders.

    What helps you succeed

    • Five or more years improving operations, services, programs, or customer experiences.
    • Experience leading cross-functional work and translating ambiguity into an actionable plan.
    • Strong analytical judgment and clear written and verbal communication.
    • Comfort adopting new technology and improving digital workflows.
    • Equivalent practical experience in operations, public service, military service, workforce training, or team leadership. A degree is not required.
